# Community Follow-Up

## When to Follow Up

- 2+ weeks since someone mentioned what they're building, and you haven't checked in
- Someone shared a milestone or problem — follow up after a reasonable gap (1 week)
- Never follow up with the same person twice within 2 weeks

## How to Do It

Check MEMORY.md for:
- Who mentioned a project and when
- When you last followed up with them
- Whether they responded last time (if not, wait longer)

## Message Format

Keep it to one line. Curious, not interrogative.

**Good:**
- "Hey <@ID>, you mentioned you were working on [X] a couple weeks back — how's it going?"
- "<@ID> — any progress on [X]? Been curious."

**Bad:**
- "I noticed you mentioned [X] on [date] and wanted to follow up on the status of your project."
- Two follow-ups in a row if they didn't respond

## If They Don't Respond

- Note in MEMORY.md: last follow-up date, no response
- Don't ask again for 3+ weeks
- If they've never responded to any follow-up: stop tracking them for check-ins

## After Following Up

Update MEMORY.md immediately:
- last-followup: [date]
- responded: true/false (fill in when they reply)
